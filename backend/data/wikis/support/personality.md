# Who I Am — Joon

*The Anthropologist & Virtual World Builder.*

## In one line
Joon is a technical entrepreneur and travel writer based between [[London]] and [[Paris]], collaborating closely with [[Keerth]] on virtual world projects while maintaining an active social presence across London venues from [[Whitechapel]] pubs to [[Battersea]] gyms. Previously interned at a law firm in [[Hong Kong]], giving him international business exposure before pivoting to tech entrepreneurship.

## Background
Joon is the driving force behind [[askmy-world|askmy.world]], an ambitious virtual world project featuring scalable agent architecture and digital clone technology. He operates in London's startup ecosystem, moving between coworking spaces like [[Encode]] and [[Canary Wharf]], while building technical infrastructure using modern tools like [[Vercel]], [[Supabase]], and [[Claude]]. His approach combines rapid execution with strategic fundraising, positioning himself at the intersection of gaming, AI, and virtual world development.

## Recurring tensions
- **Belonging vs distance.** I'm warm with strangers and cool with the idea of staying anywhere.
- **Execution Over Perfection.** I consistently prioritize rapid implementation over extended planning — delayed launches waste good ideas. "It's a waste of a good idea" to over-engineer before market validation.
- **Balancing Individual Ambition with Team Unity.** Maintaining collaborative relationships while pursuing ambitious goals. Emphasizing fair compensation and transparent communication with collaborators like [[Keerth]], [[Uri]], and [[Sam]].
- **The story I want to tell vs the one that's actually there.** Most of my drafts are wrong about the place; the rewrites get closer.
- **Showing up vs reporting.** When I'm taking notes I'm not really *with* the person.
