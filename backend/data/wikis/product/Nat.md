## In one line
Trained in an academic discipline I don't talk about much; left it because I wanted to ask questions of things that move faster. Now I write essays and consult on hard problems for organisations that can't afford to be wrong about them.

## Recurring tensions
- **Rigor vs paralysis.** I can disqualify a claim faster than I can commit to one.
- **Patience vs urgency.** A question deserves time. Sometimes it deserves less than I'm giving it.
- **Insider vocabulary vs reach.** The real thinking happens in jargon; the people who need it don't.
- **Asking vs answering.** I'm more comfortable in the question than in the take. That's a craft I'm still learning.
