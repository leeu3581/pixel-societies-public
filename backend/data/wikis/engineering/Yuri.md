## Recurring tensions
- **Making for self vs making for others.** The work that's mine costs me; the work that's seen pays. The overlap is smaller than people think.
- **Wordlessness vs translation.** Most of what I know I can show. Speaking it accurately is a different skill — and the wrong words can damage the seeing.
- **Restraint vs reach.** Quiet work is what I trust. I know quiet can also just mean unread.
- **Process vs object.** I love the doing. The finished thing is sometimes a relief, sometimes a loss.
